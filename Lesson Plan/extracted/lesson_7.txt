LESSON 7: Occupational Vocabulary, Case Records, and Ticket Hygiene

A. LESSON OVERVIEW

Lesson Number & Title: Lesson 7: Occupational Vocabulary, Case Records, and Ticket Hygiene

Allocated Learning Duration: 120 Minutes (2.0 Hours)

Occupational Relevance: In multinational BPO contact centers and IT helpdesks in Malaysia (e.g., Cogoport JA015, Scicom JA020, Corebase Technologies JA022, BASF JA010, Concentrix JA003), every voice call or webchat must be documented in a Customer Relationship Management (CRM) ticketing platform (such as Zendesk, Salesforce, or ServiceNow). Incomplete, sloppy, or informal ticket records cause confusion during shift handovers, violate audit compliance, and inflate Average Handling Time (AHT). Frontline specialists must master contact center acronyms (SLA, FCR, AHT, TAT, CSAT) and write concise, professional CRM case notes using standardized past-tense diagnostic verbs.

Measurable Learning Objectives:By the end of this 120-minute lesson, Thai IT undergraduate learners will be able to:

Define and apply core contact center acronyms (SLA, FCR, AHT, TAT, CSAT, CRM) correctly within occupational support contexts.

Construct standardized past-tense diagnostic case log entries ("Verified credentials, isolated authentication failure, reconfigured gateway settings, and resolved ticket") using professional workplace formatting.

Edit and refine sloppy, informal, or incomplete call notes into polished, audit-compliant CRM ticket records.

Produce a complete, grammatically accurate written CRM ticket log from a raw customer interaction transcript within 15 minutes, achieving 100% compliance with industry ticket hygiene standards.

D. EXPLICIT TEACHING OF SENTENCE STRUCTURES AND LANGUAGE USE

1. Language Pattern 1: Core Contact Center Acronyms & Jargon

========================================================================================

LANGUAGE PATTERN 1: CONTACT CENTER ACRONYMS & JARGON

========================================================================================

Form / Concept Definition & Usage:

  - SLA (Service Level Agreement): Contractual resolution deadline (e.g., "P2 SLA = 2 Hours").

  - FCR (First Contact Resolution): Case resolved on first call without callback ("FCR Achieved").

  - AHT (Average Handling Time): Total duration of call + wrap-up logging ("Target AHT = 300s").

  - TAT (Turnaround Time): Time elapsed from ticket opening to final closure.

  - CSAT (Customer Satisfaction): Customer rating score following interaction.

  - CRM (Customer Relationship Management): System used to log tickets (e.g., Zendesk, Salesforce).



Communicative Function:

  Using industry-standard terminology in BPO ticket logs and team performance reporting.



Explanation in Thai (คำอธิบายภาษาไทย):

  การทำงานในศูนย์ BPO และ Shared Service Center ในประเทศมาเลเซีย จำเป็นต้องบันทึกข้อมูลโดยใช้คำย่อมาตรฐานสากล เพื่อความสะดวกรวดเร็วและเป็นมืออาชีพ การเข้าใจคำย่อเหล่านี้ช่วยให้เขียนบันทึก CRM ได้อย่างกระชับและผ่านเกณฑ์การตรวจสอบ Quality Audit



Contextualized Workplace Examples:

  Example 1: "Case resolved on initial contact. Marked as FCR in CRM portal."

  Example 2: "Ticket escalated to Tier-2 due to pending SLA breach (TAT: 1.5 hours)."

  Example 3: "Customer expressed high satisfaction (CSAT 5/5) following password reset guidance."



Common Learner Errors & Corrections:

  x Incorrect: "Finish problem in first time call." (Wordy and non-standard)

    ✓ Correct: "Case resolved upon initial call (FCR achieved)."

  x Incorrect: "Late time for contract promise." (Incorrect workplace vocabulary)

    ✓ Correct: "Ticket escalated to prevent SLA breach."

2. Language Pattern 2: Past-Tense Diagnostic Verbs for CRM Logging

========================================================================================

LANGUAGE PATTERN 2: PAST-TENSE DIAGNOSTIC VERBS FOR CRM LOGS

========================================================================================

Form / Syntax Pattern:

  [Field Header: Action Taken:] + [Past-Tense Diagnostic Verb: Verified / Isolated / Guided / Reconfigured / Escalated / Resolved] + [Technical Component]



Communicative Function:

  Recording concise, objective, past-tense summaries of actions taken during a customer support call in CRM ticket logs.



Explanation in Thai (คำอธิบายภาษาไทย):

  การเขียนบันทึก CRM ในช่อง Action Taken นิยมใช้กริยาช่อง 2 (Past-Tense Verbs) ที่แสดงถึงการกระทำทางเทคนิคที่เสร็จสิ้นแล้ว ขึ้นต้นประโยคแบบย่อ (Telegraphic style) โดยไม่จำเป็นต้องใส่ประธาน "I" หรือ "The agent" เพื่อความกระชับและเป็นทางการ



Contextualized Workplace Examples:

  Example 1: "Verified customer identity via OTP. Isolated issue to corrupted browser cache."

  Example 2: "Guided user through network adapter reset. Confirmed connection restored."

  Example 3: "Reconfigured VPN server gateway settings. Issued new authentication token."

  Example 4: "Escalated ticket to Tier-2 Engineering due to database timeout error."



Common Learner Errors & Corrections:

  x Incorrect: "I am helping customer change password and it work." (Present tense & informal)

    ✓ Correct: "Guided customer through password reset. Verified login functional."

  x Incorrect: "Customer tell app crash so I check." (Broken grammar)

    ✓ Correct: "Customer reported application crash. Isolated fault to memory leak."

RESOLUTION & CURRENT STATUS:

- RESOLVED. User successfully exported monthly report without system freeze.

- FCR achieved (AHT: 8.5 minutes).

- No further customer action required. Ticket closed.
