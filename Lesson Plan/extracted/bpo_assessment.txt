DELIVERABLE 3: ASSESSMENT PACKAGE (PARALLEL PRE-TEST FORM A &amp; POST-TEST FORM B)
1. ASSESSMENT PACKAGE OVERVIEW &amp; ADMINISTRATION MANUAL
1.1 Purpose and Target Audience
This Occupational English for Specific Purposes (EOP) Assessment Package is designed to measure the oral communicative competence of Thai IT undergraduate students and frontline customer support/service desk specialists seeking employment in Malaysian BPO contact centers (e.g., Concentrix, Teleperformance, TDCX, Scicom, Accenture, BASF).
The package consists of two parallel assessment forms:
Form A (Pre-Test / Diagnostic Assessment): Administered prior to instruction to establish a diagnostic baseline of oral language proficiency across workplace communicative tasks.
Form B (Post-Test / Post-Intervention Assessment): Administered upon completion of the 19-hour instructional module to evaluate learning gain, task mastery, and occupational readiness.
1.2 Test Construct &amp; Blueprint Alignment
Both assessment forms share identical construct validity, task specifications, difficulty weighting, and scoring criteria. They systematically measure the eight core communication functions trained across Lessons 1–8:

1.3 Standardized Administration Guidelines
Testing Environment: Quiet, computer-assisted language laboratory or online recording terminal with audio playback capability, noise-canceling headset, and microphone.
Audio Delivery: Prompt lines are delivered via standardized pre-recorded audio prompts.
Response Recording: Candidate responses must be recorded in single-take audio files without pausing or editing.
Timing Control: Preparation and speaking timers are strictly enforced by the administrator or automated software interface.

TASK 1: PROFESSIONAL CALL OPENING AND IDENTITY VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Workplace Context: OWNDAYS Malaysia Customer Service Helpline (`JA001`).
Situation: An inbound caller rings the helpline regarding an online prescription glasses order.
Inbound Customer Audio Prompt:
[AUDIO PROMPT 1.A]: &quot;Hello? Is this OWNDAYS Customer Care? I placed an order online three days ago, but I haven't received any tracking update yet.&quot;
Your Task:
Deliver a professional voice call opening. State the brand salutation, introduce yourself, express readiness to help with the tracking query, and politely ask for the caller's full name and order reference number.
- Preparation Time: 45 Seconds
- Target Speaking Duration: 25 – 35 Seconds
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TASK 2: DIAGNOSTIC FAULT ISOLATION AND REFLECTIVE PARAPHRASING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Workplace Context: Level-1 Technical Service Desk at Concentrix Malaysia (`JA003`).
Situation: A business client calls reporting that their mobile banking portal is freezing.
Inbound Customer Audio Prompt:
[AUDIO PROMPT 2.A]: &quot;Hi, helpdesk! Your banking portal is completely useless! I tried logging in to process an urgent vendor payment, but the screen just freezes and won't let me in!&quot;
Your Task:
Acknowledge the caller's frustration calmly. Ask two indirect diagnostic questions to isolate whether the issue is caused by an error code or network connection type (Wi-Fi vs. Mobile Data). Finish with a reflective paraphrasing check to confirm your understanding.
- Preparation Time: 45 Seconds
- Target Speaking Duration: 35 – 45 Seconds
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TASK 3: TECHNICAL WORKAROUND AND EMPATHETIC DE-ESCALATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Workplace Context: Customer Complaint Operations at Teleperformance Malaysia (`JA004`).
Situation: An irate merchant calls because their payment checkout button is unresponsive, causing lost sales.
Inbound Customer Audio Prompt:
[AUDIO PROMPT 3.A]: &quot;This is unacceptable! My customers are trying to pay on my online store, but the checkout button is completely dead! I am losing hundreds of dollars every hour and nobody is fixing it!&quot;
Your Task:
De-escalate the caller using empathetic validation and a formal corporate apology. Offer a technical workaround in plain English using First Conditional logic (&quot;If you clear your browser cookies...&quot;). Reassure personal case ownership and ask them to test the fix while you stay connected.
- Preparation Time: 60 Seconds
- Target Speaking Duration: 45 – 60 Seconds
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TASK 4: CRM TICKET SYNTHESIS AND ORAL SHIFT HANDOVER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Workplace Context: Global Shift Handover Meeting at Accenture Malaysia (`JA013`).
Situation: You are completing your shift and must deliver an oral handover speech to the incoming shift lead based on the CRM ticket record below.
CRM Ticket Record (#TK-9021):
- Account: Accenture Order-to-Cash (OTC) Regional Portal
- Priority: P1 Critical (Regional Impact: Thailand Desk)
- Symptom: Automated payment reconciliation module freeze during batch processing; Error Code '504 Timeout'.
- Root Cause: Database table lock congestion following overnight patch update.
- Actions Completed: Tier-1 executed session reset, flushed DNS cache, and isolated blocked tables. Activated temporary manual batch workaround.
- Current Status: P1 Escalated Status with Senior Database Engineering Pod.
- Required Next Step: Incoming lead must verify database table re-indexing with Tier-2 at 09:30 AM and update client prior to 10:00 AM SLA breach deadline.
Your Task:
Deliver a structured 90-second Oral Case Summary using the 5-Stage Handover Framework (Identification -&gt; Symptom -&gt; Actions Completed -&gt; Current Status -&gt; Next Step/SLA).
- Preparation Time: 90 Seconds
- Target Speaking Duration: 75 – 90 Seconds

2.3 Form A Benchmark Model Responses &amp; Performance Indicators
Task 1 Model Response (Call Opening)
&quot;Thank you for calling OWNDAYS Customer Care. My name is Golf. I would be glad to check your order tracking status today. To access your account details securely, may I please verify your full name and order reference number?&quot;
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
Brand name salutation (OWNDAYS Customer Care) + Agent name (Golf).
Polite offer of help (&quot;I would be glad to check...&quot;).
Modal credential request (&quot;May I please verify...&quot;).
Task 2 Model Response (Fault Isolation)
&quot;I understand how frustrating it is when your portal freezes during an urgent payment. I can certainly help you look into this right now. Could you tell me if any error code displays when the screen freezes? Also, would you mind specifying whether you are connected via Wi-Fi or cellular mobile data? So if I understand correctly, the portal freezes upon login while connected to Wi-Fi, is that right?&quot;
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
Empathetic validation without panic.
Indirect diagnostic questions (&quot;Could you tell me if...&quot;, &quot;Would you mind specifying whether...&quot;).
Statement word order after indirect lead-ins.
Reflective paraphrasing check (&quot;So if I understand correctly...&quot;).
Task 3 Model Response (Workaround &amp; De-escalation)
&quot;I completely understand how critical this payment checkout issue is for your business, and I agree that losing sales is unacceptable. On behalf of Teleperformance, I sincerely apologize for this inconvenience. Rest assured that I am taking personal ownership of your case right now. This glitch occurs because old browser cookies are blocking the payment gateway. If you clear your browser cookies and refresh the page, the checkout button will function properly. Please try clearing your cookies now, and I will stay on the line to verify if it works.&quot;
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
HEAR Framework application (Validation + Formal Apology).
Personal ownership (&quot;Rest assured that I am taking personal ownership...&quot;).
Plain English cause explanation + First Conditional workaround (If + present, will + base verb).
Collaborative verification request.
Task 4 Model Response (CRM Synthesis &amp; Handover)
&quot;Good morning, team. I am briefing you on Ticket ID #TK-9021, a Priority 1 Critical incident affecting the Accenture Order-to-Cash regional portal. The client reported a complete system freeze during batch payment reconciliation, displaying Error Code 504 Timeout, impacting the Thailand desk. Initial diagnostic checks confirmed that the outage was caused by database table lock congestion following an overnight patch update. To date, Tier-1 has executed a session reset, flushed the DNS cache, and isolated the blocked tables, while activating a temporary manual processing workaround. The case is currently sitting at P1 Escalated Status with our Senior Database Engineering Pod. The required next step is for the incoming shift lead to verify database table re-indexing with Tier-2 at 09:30 AM and update the client prior to our 10:00 AM SLA breach deadline. Thank you.&quot;
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
Complete coverage of all 5 stages in 80–85 seconds.
Ticket-to-speech transition markers (&quot;I am briefing you on...&quot;, &quot;Initial diagnostic checks confirmed...&quot;, &quot;sitting at P1 status&quot;, &quot;The required next step is...&quot;).
Accurate inclusion of SLA deadline (10:00 AM) and ticket ID (#TK-9021).
