1. Assessment Overview, Construct &amp; Administration Guidelines
1.1 Purpose and Target AudienceThis Occupational English for Specific Purposes (EOP) Assessment Package is engineered to evaluate the oral communicative competence of Thai undergraduate IT students preparing for frontline customer support and technical service desk positions in Malaysian Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) contact centers and Shared Service Centres (SSCs) (e.g., Concentrix, Teleperformance, TDCX, Scicom, Accenture, BASF). The assessment package consists of two 100% parallel assessment forms: Form A (Pre-Test / Diagnostic Baseline Assessment) and Form B (Post-Test / Post-Intervention Summative Assessment). Both forms measure candidate proficiency across identical task types, difficulty weightings, preparation time budgets, and scoring criteria to calculate exact pre-to-post learning gain scores.
1.2 Assessment Construct &amp; Blueprint MatrixCandidates listen to standardized prerecorded customer audio prompts over headsets and deliver spoken responses under strict time limits. 1.3 Standardized Administration Guidelines• Testing Environment: Quiet, computer-assisted language laboratory or online recording terminal with audio playback capability, noise-canceling USB headset, and microphone positioned 2 cm from the candidate's mouth.• Audio Prompt Delivery: All customer prompt lines are delivered via pre-recorded audio prompts to eliminate rater/actor variability and ensure 100% standardized testing conditions.• Single-Take Recording Rule: Candidate spoken responses must be recorded in single-take audio files without pausing, re-recording, or post-editing.• Strict Time Control: Preparation and speaking timers are strictly enforced by the administrator or automated software interface.

2.3 Form A Benchmark Model Responses &amp; Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Task 1 Model Response (Call Opening):"Thank you for calling OWNDAYS Customer Care. My name is Golf. I would be glad to check your order tracking status today. To access your account details securely, may I please verify your full name and order reference number?"Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):• Brand name salutation (OWNDAYS Customer Care) + Agent name (Golf).• Polite offer of help ("I would be glad to check...").• Modal credential request ("May I please verify...").
Task 2 Model Response (Fault Isolation):"I understand how frustrating it is when your portal freezes during an urgent payment. I can certainly help you look into this right now. Could you tell me if any error code displays when the screen freezes? Also, would you mind specifying whether you are connected via Wi-Fi or cellular mobile data? So if I understand correctly, the portal freezes upon login while connected to Wi-Fi, is that right?"Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):• Empathetic validation without panic.• Indirect diagnostic questions ("Could you tell me if...", "Would you mind specifying whether...").• Statement word order after indirect lead-ins.• Reflective paraphrasing check ("So if I understand correctly...").
Task 3 Model Response (Troubleshooting &amp; De-escalation):"I completely understand how critical this payment checkout issue is for your business, and I agree that losing sales is unacceptable. On behalf of Teleperformance, I sincerely apologize for this inconvenience. Rest assured that I am taking personal ownership of your case right now. This glitch occurs because old browser cookies are blocking the payment gateway. If you clear your browser cookies and refresh the page, the checkout button will function properly. Please try clearing your cookies now, and I will stay on the line to verify if it works."Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):• HEAR Framework application (Validation + Formal Apology).• Personal ownership ("Rest assured that I am taking personal ownership...").• Plain English cause explanation + First Conditional workaround (If + present, will + base verb).• Collaborative verification request.
Task 4 Model Response (CRM Synthesis &amp; Oral Shift Handover):"Good morning, team. I am briefing you on Ticket ID #TK-9021, a Priority 1 Critical incident affecting the Accenture Order-to-Cash regional portal. The client reported a complete system freeze during batch payment reconciliation, displaying Error Code 504 Timeout, impacting the Thailand desk. Initial diagnostic checks confirmed that the outage was caused by database table lock congestion following an overnight patch update. To date, Tier-1 has executed a session reset, flushed the DNS cache, and isolated the blocked tables, while activating a temporary manual processing workaround. The case is currently sitting at P1 Escalated Status with our Senior Database Engineering Pod. The required next step is for the incoming shift lead to verify database table re-indexing with Tier-2 at 09:30 AM and update the client prior to our 10:00 AM SLA breach deadline. Thank you."Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):• Complete coverage of all 5 stages in 80–85 seconds.• Ticket-to-speech transition markers ("I am briefing you on...", "Initial diagnostic checks confirmed...", "sitting at P1 status", "The required next step is...").• Accurate inclusion of SLA deadline (10:00 AM) and ticket ID (#TK-9021).
