4. SECTION 3: METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS &amp; SCOPE RECORD
4.1 Study Boundaries for Thesis Inclusion
To maintain academic rigor and transparency in Chapter 3 of the thesis, three explicit study boundaries are recorded 9, 25, 26:
Asynchronous Simulated Task Limitation: The pre-test and post-test instruments evaluate spoken production against standardized pre-recorded audio prompts rather than live, spontaneous, reciprocal customer negotiations 26. While this design guarantees identical testing conditions for all participants, it does not measure real-time conversational improvisation 26.
Human-Rater Evaluation Scope: Spoken audio recordings are evaluated by trained human raters using the 20-point EOP Analytic Oral Proficiency Rubric rather than Automated Speech Recognition (ASR) software 26. Rater calibration sessions are conducted prior to scoring to establish high inter-rater reliability (Intraclass Correlation Coefficient \\(ICC &gt; 0.85\\)).
Conceptual Parallel Equivalence Assumption: Form A and Form B are engineered to be parallel in scenario complexity, prompt length, information load, and target language structures 22, 26. Empirical parallel-form equivalence (\\(r &gt; 0.85\\)) will be statistically verified following pilot testing (\\(N = 20\\) pilot students) 26, 27.

6. SECTION 5: REPRODUCIBLE RESEARCH PROTOCOL &amp; PROMPT AUDIT TRAIL
6.1 Data Screening &amp; Inclusion Rules
During job advertisement analysis, data screening followed strict operational rules to eliminate selection bias 5, 28:
Geographic Location Rule: Job postings must be located in Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, Penang, Cyberjaya) 28.
Relevant Duties Ratio Formula:\\\text{Calculated Ratio (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Support Bullets } (n)}{\text{Total Bullets } (N_{\text{total}})} \right) \times 100\\
Support Bullets (\\(n\\)): Count only explicit bullet points describing customer service, helpline interaction, technical troubleshooting, order processing, or ticketing 29, 30.
Total Bullets (\\(N_{\text{total}}\\)): Count all identifiable job-duty bullets, excluding company overviews, qualification requirements, benefits, and general admin headers 30.
Inclusion Threshold: Positions were included only if the Calculated Ratio was strictly greater than 50.0% (\\(&gt; 50.0\%\\)) 5, 31. Postings with a ratio of exactly 50.0% or less (\\(\le 50.0\%\\)) were excluded to maintain a strict majority threshold 31.
